# Prediction markets as partially observed concurrent economic games

**Owning section:** prediction-market engineering. **Case study:** AAA retail gasoline directional markets on Kalshi. **Status:** research doctrine and working source modules; no new admitted live trading lane or certified edge.

## 1. A prediction is not yet a market strategy

There are at least five different products of information:

1. A forecast of the eventual **official reported settlement** (including its exact source, vintage, revision/rounding rules and publication clock).
2. A proof or refutation that a payout remains **admissible** under precisely stated, source-aligned evidence.
3. An assessment of which offers on a central limit order book are **jointly coherent** and executable, rather than just plausible midpoints.
4. A policy for offering liquidity in a **concurrent game against adaptive traders**, with conditional fills, queue priority and inventory exposure.
5. An opportunity to earn **eligibility-dependent liquidity rewards** even if no order fills, against an evolving competitor population and rules for excluded scoring snapshots.

A model may be excellent at (1) and lose money at (4). A structurally inconsistent ladder in (3) need not yield cash when legs cannot fill together. A market-maker reward in (5) is not forecast alpha and may be nil despite outstanding orders.

The research objective is to maximize realized incremental net cash per permitted action and unit of computation, not model novelty, nominal prediction accuracy, model confidence, quote counts, or an agent's number of attempted trades.

## 2. The four interacting clocks and the governing evidence order

Define X as the underlying evolving physical/economic process. Source S publishes M_S(X) using measurement, rounding, reporting and revision conventions. Contract j resolves using settlement function Y_j = R_j(M_S(X), rules_j), not necessarily direct observation of X.

At a decision time t, the only admissible information set is F_t, generated by public messages whose **received availability time** is no later than t, together with market prices, order book and active rules available at t. Record, separately:

- event occurrence/observation timestamp;
- source first-received timestamp (as opposed to its retrospective source "as of" date);
- exchange quote/trade received timestamp;
- order-placement, acknowledgment, fill and cancellation timestamps;
- venue close, source publication and authoritative settlement timestamps.

The same data can support different levels of assertion: observed, source-aligned, calibrated and trade-admitted. The four-valued evidence carrier is the existing SignCell/SignChain pipeline, with the mathematical semantics of FOUR and modal/closure operators owned by Lenticule's Obsmetrologia. It must preserve support FOR, support AGAINST, contradictory support for both, and absence of evidence. An empty admissible-world set due to contradictory reports never authorizes explosion.

A source observation after market close cannot be retroactively turned into a pre-close tradable strategy. Original rule text/hash, source population, date, strike, measurement and vintage must match exactly.

## 3. The common mathematical stack, in ownership order

### Obsmetrologia (existing)

FOUR bilattice, both truth and knowledge orders, modal reachability, closure, and the distinction between a proof conditional on model axioms and evidence those axioms physically hold. No duplicate four-valued logic in the venue.

### Existing gaming and market-game libraries (existing)

Concurrent opponent moves, accepted plays, position/inventory state, scenario payoff operators, evidence-conditioned scenario likelihood updates, scored study/replay, multi-strategy portfolio allocation and venue custody boundaries. An informed competitor changes quotes or crosses our quotes while we calculate: intelligence arrives in a *game*, not a fixed table.

### Prediction-market engineering (upstream of venues)

- settlement-modal: interval and continuous-path admissibility deductions; assumptions may not be inferred from empirical percentiles.
- binary-maker-game: sharp Fréchet bounds on settlement-and-fill dependence, with minimax abstention and optional explicitly uncertain reward scenarios.
- market-game: existing discrete **joint scenarios**, likelihood conditioning and payoff/position integration.

All are research operators and do not sign orders, decide venue tick sizes, fetch Kalshi accounts, or mint competing kinds of money.

### Kalshi (downstream venue-specific consumers)

- coherence-families, coherence, simplex and exact existing fee schedule: already own strike interval covers, set containment, joint feasible distributions, and conditional Dutch-book LP portfolios. Do not reimplement them for gas.
- aaa-gas-market-game: binds original AAA contractual text, geography/population, event date and strike; projects upstream weighted joint scenarios to exact "price > strike" binary payouts and their **nested covariance**; ranks separately provided scored maker fills via upstream robust coupling. It does not own gasoline physics, Bayesian inference, book custody or trade execution.
- liquidity-incentives: existing Kalshi LIP terms/reference-price computation, now with two-sided instantaneous normalized share bounds, boundary-price priority uncertainty and variable subcent tick size. This is neither a period-wide denominator forecast nor a receivable credit.

An alternative venue must bind its own settlement rules, quote semantics, fee/reward mechanism and custody. It consumes the shared math but must not copy Kalshi's specifics as if universal.

## 4. A gas-price source model worth testing

AAA's daily national and regional average of regular gasoline is published using OPIS survey data; AAA describes up to 120,000 observed fuel stations in cooperation with WEX. The AAA national aggregate is NOT certified to equal an unweighted average of state aggregates. Treat national, each state and each daily/weekly/monthly contract as separately source-scoped measurements. Retail price is not NYMEX RBOB, Brent, crude spot price or an EIA weekly survey.

Let i denote a named AAA survey population, t a target publication day, W upstream wholesale/rack/RBOB prices, Z observable refinery/supply/inventory/weather shocks and z_t a latent adjustment regime. A candidate asymmetric, pooled error-correction/state-space family is:

    R(i,t) = R(i,t-1) + mu(i,z_t)
            + sum_{l=0}^L [ beta+(i,l,z_t) * max(dW(i,t-l),0)
                           + beta-(i,l,z_t) * min(dW(i,t-l),0) ]
            + lambda(i)^T Z(t) + epsilon(i,t).

Mix over latent regimes (calm, abrupt positive cost shock, negative cost shock, local refinery interruption, reporting revision, etc.), learning transitions from *past* source receipts. Use hierarchical shrinkage across states rather than giving each state a poorly estimated independent model. Include day-of-week, holiday, publication-age and available wholesale inventory information where demonstrated useful. Nothing requires symmetry beta+ = beta-. Research on oil-to-pump pass-through documents nonlinear asymmetric adjustments and inaction bands; those are candidate features, not evidence that this model beats Kalshi.

More sophisticated modeling options are a hierarchical hidden Markov model, particle filter, calibrated conformal residual intervals, dynamic factor covariance, and physical pipeline/stock-flow constraints. Their value is an incremental, falsifiable reduction in the conditional uncertainty of **tomorrow's actual AAA source number** at an issuance time before the venue closes.

If any model uses a satellite/retail sign image, station trace or paid source data, its receipt timestamp and usage rights must be recorded. An aftermarket revision is not known to the earlier trader. Historical AAA archive values are outcome labels only once their vintage was actually published.

## 5. Joint scenario forecast -> coherent market ladders

For a single reported gas-price variable G and increasing strikes k_1 < k_2:

    Y_j = 1(G > k_j),
    Y_2 <= Y_1 in every source-consistent world.

A shared weighted scenario law {G_s, w_s} produces:

    p_j = sum_s w_s * 1(G_s > k_j)
    P(Y_i=1 AND Y_j=1) = p_max(i,j)
    Cov(Y_i,Y_j) = p_max(i,j) - p_i p_j.

A pair of contract outcomes on the same AAA price is *not independent*. This covariance matters for both market-making inventory and portfolio funding. Nor is it valid to treat the national survey and a state survey as the same G.

The Kalshi AAA adapter implements this exact pushforward from the existing market-game scenario record. It does not independently learn the posterior weights or grant the result calibration standing. The existing coherence solver independently tests whether *market quotes* admit any distribution obeying the threshold relations, using the executable bid/ask and displayed depth. A structural gap without simultaneous fills is a **potential** Dutch book, not cash.

## 6. Passive orders: distinguish expected edge from fill-conditional edge

Let W be the event that the side we buy wins, F that our post-only order fills, p = P(W), f = P(F), and c the acquired contract's price plus actual maker fee per fill. Expected dollars **per placement**:

    E[Pi] = P(W intersect F) - c*f + E[incentive] - carry.

The joint event is not known from marginals. Fréchet-Hoeffding yields the SHARP bounds:

    max(0,p+f-1) <= P(W intersect F) <= min(p,f).

The shared binary-maker-game now optimizes the resulting P&L across valid p and f uncertainty bands, including their piecewise breakpoints; it ranks against doing nothing. Any attractive "predictive" edge should be stress-tested against these dependency bounds, a separately measured queue-conditioned fill process, and an actual realized fill ledger.

Gas tape from October 4 showed dramatically different outcomes for clean fills versus sweeps through the bid, and the existing daily/weekly autoregressions were worse than the Kalshi midpoint. This motivates modeling *who fills* and *when*, rather than treating every one-hour quote drift or maker touch as monetizable alpha.

Next empirical modeling: queue-aware competing-risks hazards (a resting order can fill cleanly, be swept through, be canceled ahead of us, time out, or be pulled), markouts at 5/30/120s, source-arrival regimes, and rival-information classes. A Hawkes/self-exciting arrival model may help in bursty books; a state-dependent survival or ordinal hazard baseline should be tested first. Only recorded queue position and source-received event time validate it. There is no assumption that midprice mean reversion exceeds the spread.

For a two-sided maker, extend to the JOINT fill distribution and portfolio inventory. Two independent marginal P&Ls do not suffice: one side can fill precisely when adverse news arrives, and the other side may never fill. Use existing shared scenario payoff and portfolio constraints; do not double-count the same gas settlement twice as uncorrelated risk.

## 7. Liquidity incentives are a separate game

Kalshi's current Liquidity Incentive Program scores eligible resting book orders from random intra-second snapshots. Both YES and NO BID sides need depth up to the program target; for a qualifying snapshot, a side's reference price depends on cumulative size at one-fifth of the target, and eligible order size is discounted by tick distance. Normalized shares depend on all participating depth, not merely our contract count. Active rewards, target, discount, end time and other competitors can change.

The Kalshi liquidity-incentives reader now bounds our **one observed snapshot** score from aggregate depth on both sides, including unknown priority within a partially qualifying price level and subcent tick precision. Reward actually earned during a full time period is unknown from one snapshot; store it as an unanswered economic measurement until receipts exist. Program enrollment/eligibility, excluded seconds, paying member status, random snapshots and actual paid credits must be verified separately.

A dedicated liquidity-provider agreement is distinct from the regular Liquidity Incentive Program. Do not assume one gives the privileges or payout of the other.

Test a reward-farming hypothesis only with contemporaneously captured programs, both-sided books, random-sample sensitivity, participant share bounds, and eventually paid reward receipts. Never manufacture a guaranteed incentive income from the displayed pool or place orders merely to alter the reference price/manipulate the scoring mechanism.

## 8. Information value and the opponent's learning

For an information action a (fetch a source, fit a model, query a higher-frequency sensor, rerun a particle filter), the *gross* expected value of sample information under a fixed action set is:

    EVSI = E_S[max_u E[U(u,omega)|S]] - max_u E[U(u,omega)].

This cannot be negative before information cost if the agent may ignore S. But it may be close to zero, and a concurrent market opponent can reprice before our order arrives. For a real trading cycle optimize incremental value **net** of compute, API use, quote latency, spread, fees, inventory carry and adverse fill. Do not buy new data unless prospective conservative incremental value exceeds these costs.

The strategy set should include:
- maker one-sided, maker two-sided and LIP-directed measured resting orders;
- taker against another's displayed quote;
- cross-strike structural portfolio and multi-market hedge;
- hold inventory to settlement, early exit, inventory rebalance or abstain;
- event-driven belief updating and quote cancellation upon new public data;
- weather, gas, diesel, energy, inflation, rates and sports treated as different source operators on the SAME market-game substrate.

An agent's earlier analysis should constrain later actions through typed evidence, not an unstructured persona's internal narrative. Different agents can act as market opponent, forecaster, physical-science verifier, fee/quote engineer, and independent receiver within the same game. These roles share market evidence without granting one another execution authority.

## 9. Scientific controls, actual prior results and negative evidence

The earlier historical AAA daily AR lost to Kalshi's market midpoint on held-out Brier (daily 0.0857 vs market 0.0387; weekly 0.3531 vs 0.1322). The recorded taker performance of the daily AR was negative. A separate belief-path study across seven classes found no fee-surviving generic round-trip path lane. This is an explicit null benchmark to beat, not evidence of a missing magic one-line coefficient.

A new 2026-10-12 point-in-time collector audit uses immutable October 3-4 source files:
- 71 polling ticks produced TWO distinct national AAA source date/value versions;
- 886 comparable, pre-close displayed gas-threshold bid/ask pairs contained ZERO higher-strike bids strictly above lower-strike asks;
- no book depths or exact per-tick rule hashes were retained, so it is NOT a fee/depth-certified arbitrage scan;
- repeated polls of the same day/source do not make 71 independent outcome labels.

This pilot is retained in Kalshi forecasting-service/data/records/2026-10-12-aaa-gas-game/point-in-time-pilot.json. It suggests *source information arrival* may be much sparser than poll frequency, and motivates event-driven computation.

For prospective verification, freeze models and selection windows before evaluation. Split by independent settlement day (and state family), not by minute or rung; adjacent 5-minute quotes on a day are highly dependent. Track source publication latency and correct rules. Measure Brier, log loss, CRPS, calibrated near-threshold tails, predictive ranking vs executable book prices, net P&L **per placement**, depth-replay fill rate, bad-sweep fraction and inventory drawdown. Cluster uncertainty by settlement event/date, add regime stress and multiple-comparison correction. Reconcile *paid* incentives and actual fills from independent receivers.

A strategy can enter live operation only after independent receiving of a measured positive lower confidence bound net of all costs, coherent rules, fresh inputs, and an admitted bounded authority envelope. Existing custody and signer remain authoritative; research creates no new actuator.

## 10. Research sequence and owners

**First, durable baselines:** keep the existing calibration, AAA collector records, gas microstructure and coherent book scan. Repair capture of individual quote/book source times, full depth, all active incentive terms, and received source vintages. Confirm the source collector is operating before assuming an information lead.

**Second, strongest low-cost physical model:** fit asymmetric hierarchical wholesale-to-AAA distribution and compare to the original AR, simple persistence and Kalshi's executable prices. The expected advantage is regime discovery, *not* automatic AR generalization. Include an inaction band and local factory/refinery features only if held-out scores improve.

**Third, competitive maker science:** use the same frozen joint scenarios across all gas strikes; compute robust fill-selection bands, queue hazards, conditional markouts, incentive share ranges and correlated inventory. Pick between abstention, single-side and two-side quotes according to **cash per attempted placement**, not apparent midpoint reversion.

**Fourth, structural games:** run existing interval-ladder coherence and exact per-order fees under fresh depth; score candidate Dutch books by the observed probability that all required legs can fill before the market moves. No trading signal from a price-only contradiction.

**Fifth, expand deliberately:** conditional source operators for diesel, RBOB/Brent/WTI, weather, CPI/releases, Fed rates and sports; keep settlement semantics separate, reuse the same maker adversarial calculus, market-game and causal receipt machinery. Source-specific empirical predictors belong to the source-domain science, not cloned into every venue.

**Sixth, independent promotion:** publish the falsifiers and receiving receipts. Negative results should retire experiments; positive results should accrete as verified reusable capabilities.
